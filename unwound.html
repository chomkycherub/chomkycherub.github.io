Unwound

*Unwound* is a surreal stealth-horror game set in a miniature toy city. You play as a wind-up mouse in a world where decay and care intertwine.

Game, Narrative, Sound Designer, Programmer

Developed as part of the THU x King mentorship program, *Unwound* explores intimacy through atmosphere and rhythm-based tension. I designed and coded all core mechanics, created the narrative system, and composed the main soundtrack.
