Unwound

A surreal toybox horror about care and decay.

Role: Lead Game Designer, Narrative & Sound, Programming

Tools

  • Unity
  • Garageband
  • Custom editor tools

The Challenge

How to convey a dark Grimm storybook narrative with the weight of being a toy tied to a winding mechanism? How to create playful, tension-building mechanics and design surreal, eerie environments through worldbuilding, fixed cameras and sound design?

My Process

1. Design Foundations

I started with paper prototypes and free-writing to define themes of dependency, fear and loss. The idea of a winding toy constrained by time shaped early design pillars and visual direction.

2. Systems & Implementation

I built a prototype in Unity with a stamina-like winding mechanic, dynamic camera zones and modular dialogue tools. A custom editor allowed quick iteration on collectible sound triggers and level lighting states.

3. Narrative & Audio

I composed ambient music with Garageband’s toy instruments and designed spatial SFX to suggest off-screen threats. Dialogues were written in a poetic, fragmented tone, delivered through an in-game terminal system.

Outcome

The project is currently in MVP state. It allowed me to explore how narrative, mechanics and aesthetics intertwine to build a cohesive mood. It also marked my first venture into creating in-editor sound tools, which I now use across projects.
